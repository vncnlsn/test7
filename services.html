Title & Leasing

Ownership clarity before commitments are made.

APS investigates surface and mineral ownership, traces complex chains of title, and organizes the findings needed for negotiations, leasing and acquisition.

Representative scope
  • Surface and mineral ownership
  • Chain-of-title and historical record research
  • Probate, heirship and fractional interests
  • Title curative and title opinions
  • Lease research, negotiation and acquisition

02 / 04

GIS

Bring the records back to the land.

APS develops maps and project databases that connect legal descriptions, ownership, infrastructure and changing project boundaries. As field conditions or alignments change, the information can be reconciled across disciplines.

Representative scope
  • Project maps and exhibits
  • Tract platting and legal descriptions
  • Spatial data compilation
  • Surface and mineral data reconciliation
  • Project database management

03 / 04

Seismic Permitting

Access built around real conditions on the ground.

APS supports seismic and other complex field programs by coordinating landowner contact, access terms, permits, compensation and field requirements.

Representative scope
  • 2D, 3D, 4D, microseismic and VSP programs
  • Landowner contact and access agreements
  • Municipal, county, state and federal approvals
  • Permit status tracking and reporting
  • Surface damage and field coordination

04 / 04

Right-of-Way Acquisition

Keep corridors moving, tract by tract.

APS works through ownership, encumbrances, negotiations and agreements for linear infrastructure. Route adjustments and property constraints are coordinated with the supporting title and mapping information.

Representative scope
  • Permanent and temporary easements
  • Ingress, egress and workspace agreements
  • Corridor ownership and encumbrance review
  • Landowner negotiation and compensation
  • Agreement execution, tracking and reporting
