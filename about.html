The company

Decades in the field. Still close to the work.

APS began in 1980 with a practical understanding of what it takes to move land projects forward. Today, that experience continues to shape how we work with clients and landowners.

Our background

Built on experience, not distance.

Across hundreds of projects and thousands of negotiations, APS has worked through complex ownership, access and field challenges throughout North America.

The company became APS Land Services in August 2026. The name reflects a broader range of work while carrying forward the practical standards that shaped the business: direct communication, careful research and accountability for the details.

APS works across private, state, federal and tribal lands, supporting energy and infrastructure projects with research, GIS, permitting and acquisition expertise.
